What is the Pamodzi Planner?

The Pamodzi Daily Planner is a free, web-based tool designed to help you organize your tasks, track your daily activities, and stay accountable to your goals.

  • Create tasks for each day of the week
  • Track your daily activity limit (40 tasks/day)
  • Tasks automatically expire after 90 days
  • Renew important tasks monthly
  • Community-driven and completely free

Why Use This Planner?

Built by Pamodzi Brand for the community, this planner helps you build discipline, stay productive, and achieve your goals — whether you're a student, professional, or entrepreneur.
